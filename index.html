Silent Hallelujah

A quiet moment with God, every day.

Silent Hallelujah shares a short Catholic prayer, Gospel reading, Psalm or devotional each day, read slowly over peaceful nature scenes. Take a minute, breathe, and pray with us.

Scripture is quoted from the Douay-Rheims Bible. Each video's narration uses an AI voice, and every post is reviewed and approved by a person before it is published.
