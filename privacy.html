Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026 · updated 2 October 2026

This policy explains how Silent Hallelujah ("we") handles information in "Silent Hallelujah Publisher", the private tool we use to publish our own videos, and on this website.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by Silent Hallelujah to manage Silent Hallelujah's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and we do not collect personal information from viewers, followers or visitors.

What we access, and why

  • YouTube (Google) - YouTube API Services. With the channel owner's permission the tool uses three Google scopes: youtube.upload (to upload and schedule our approved videos and set their thumbnails), youtube.readonly (to read our own channel and the status and public view, like and comment counts of our own videos) and youtube.force-ssl (to manage our own playlist and to read and reply to comments on our own videos).
  • Comments on our own videos. The tool reads the comments that viewers leave on our own recent videos on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram (comment text, the commenter's display name and the comment ID). It uses them only to draft a short thank-you or prayer reply for the channel owner. A reply is posted only after the owner approves that exact reply. The tool never likes, deletes, hides or reports comments and does not look at anything else about the commenter.
  • Facebook, Instagram and TikTok - when connected, the tool publishes our approved videos to our own Page and accounts and reads the minimum account information needed to do so.

We do not access data from other YouTube channels, and we do not read private messages or contacts.

How it is stored, and for how long

Access tokens that let the tool post to our accounts are stored only on our own computer. Comment text and display names are kept in a local file on that same computer only while we reply to them, and are automatically erased after 30 days (only the opaque comment ID is kept, so that no comment is handled twice). Nothing is shared with third parties, sold, used for advertising, or used to train any model. We do not use any data for advertising.

Google API Services

Silent Hallelujah Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using YouTube features through this tool you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can revoke the tool's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This website

This site does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Deleting data

Comment text and names are erased automatically after 30 days. If you want a comment you left removed earlier, or you believe we hold information about you, email us and we will delete it. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; after revocation the tool can no longer read or post anything on the channel.
