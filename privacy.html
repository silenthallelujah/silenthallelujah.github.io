Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026

This policy explains how Silent Hallelujah ("we") handles information in "Silent Hallelujah Publisher", the private tool we use to publish our own videos, and on this website.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by Silent Hallelujah to manage Silent Hallelujah's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, and we do not collect personal information from viewers, followers or visitors.

What we access, and why

  • YouTube (Google) - with our permission, the tool uploads our approved videos to our channel and reads our own channel name to confirm it is connected to the right channel. It uses the YouTube API Services.
  • Facebook, Instagram and TikTok - when connected, the tool publishes our approved videos to our own Page and accounts and reads the minimum account information needed to do so.

We do not access, collect or store other users' data, comments, messages or contacts.

How it is stored

Access tokens that let the tool post to our accounts are stored only on our own computer, are never sold or shared, and are used only to publish our content. We do not use any data for advertising.

Google API Services

Silent Hallelujah Publisher's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using YouTube features through this tool you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can revoke the tool's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This website

This site does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Deleting data

Because the tool only stores our own access tokens, there is no user data to delete. If you believe we hold information about you, email us and we will respond and delete it.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if anything changes. Questions: silent.hallelujah.324@gmail.com
